How many hours can you work as a student in Canada?

Meskhat HossainNovember 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Almost every student I talk to asks the same thing: "How much can I work once I'm in Canada?" Working helps with living costs and gives you Canadian experience, but breaking the rules can put your status at risk. Here's what IRCC says.

The short answer

During classes: up to 24 hours per week off campus.
During scheduled breaks (like winter or summer break): unlimited hours.

This applies if you meet IRCC's eligibility requirements for working off campus. Your study permit conditions matter, so check what yours says.

What if my permit says 20 hours?

Many older study permits mention 20 hours per week. IRCC explains that if your permit says you may work 20 hours a week off campus, you're allowed to work up to 24 hours a week, as long as you continue to meet the eligibility requirements.

Can I have more than one job?

Yes. IRCC says you can work more than one job to make up your hours, as long as the total stays within the limit and you keep meeting the conditions of your study permit.

Tips to stay safe

  1. Track your weekly hours across all jobs, especially if shifts change often.
  2. Know your school's official break dates. Unlimited hours apply only during scheduled breaks.
  3. Put studies first. Your permit depends on being a student in good standing, so don't let work take over.
  4. Check IRCC's page for the full eligibility list before you start working.
