Master's scholarships in Nova Scotia.

For a master's, the money mostly goes to thesis-based (research) programs. It comes from university scholarships, teaching assistant jobs and your supervisor's research grant. Course-based master's rarely come with funding.

5universitiesDec 1earliest deadlineNov 2026last checked
1

Choose thesis-based. Almost all master's funding in Nova Scotia is for research (thesis) programs.

2

Find a supervisor first. A professor who wants you can add a stipend from their grant. Use Find My Professor to shortlist.

3

Apply in the first round. Dalhousie's $20,000 awards close Dec 1, 2026 (round 1) and Feb 28, 2027 (round 2).

5 universities

Dalhousie University

Halifax, Nova Scotia

Round 1: Dec 1, 2026

Dalhousie has $20,000 master's scholarships open to international students in thesis-based programs. Apply on the Faculty of Graduate Studies scholarship platform. A second round opens Jan 5, 2027 and closes Feb 28, 2027.

Not for most international students: The Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Master's ($27,000) is only for Canadian citizens and permanent residents.

Saint Mary's University

Halifax, Nova Scotia

Apply by Jan 31, 2027

SMU's main internal award for new research students is the Durland Graduate Research Scholarship, for incoming students in research-based master's and PhD programs. Most other funding comes from your supervisor's research grants.

  • Durland Graduate Research ScholarshipIncoming research-based master's or PhD students; Jan 31, 5:00 pm Atlantic
    Value not publishedCheck eligibility ↗

Not for most international students: CGRS Master's and Mackenzie King scholarships need Canadian citizenship, permanent residence or a Canadian degree.

Halifax, Nova Scotia

Decided in September

MSVU has one award just for international master's students, plus entrance scholarships for the thesis-based students with the highest grades. Most are decided by a committee with no application.

St. Francis Xavier University (StFX)

Antigonish, Nova Scotia

MSc Biology: Feb 15

At StFX, funding for a thesis master's is a mix of scholarships, a teaching assistantship and your supervisor's research grant, and you name your supervisor in your application. For example, StFX Biology puts living costs at $16,000 a year. If a student has no outside scholarship, the supervisor must pay a stipend of at least $5,000 a year.

  • Supervisor stipend (Biology example)When you have no external scholarship; you agree on it with your supervisor
    At least $5,000 a yearAutomatic
  • Teaching assistantshipPart of the funding mix; ask the department
    VariesAutomatic

International students pay an international fee on top of regular tuition. Agree the full funding plan with your supervisor before you apply.

Acadia University

Wolfville, Nova Scotia

Ask your department

Acadia offers Acadia Graduate Scholarships and Graduate Teaching Assistantships through each department. Acadia says funding is limited and not guaranteed. You need a GPA of 3.0/4.0 in each of your last two years.

Give yourself the best chance

01

Shortlist professors

Use Find My Professor to find 8–15 people whose recent research matches yours.

02

Email before you apply

A short, specific email with your CV and transcript. Ask if they're taking students and whether funding is available.

03

Ask what the offer includes

Funding packages mix scholarship, TA pay and supervisor stipend. Ask for the total per year and for how long.

04

Know the real cost

International tuition is higher than domestic. Check whether the package covers tuition or only part of it.

05

Apply for everything

Fill in the university's scholarship application even if your supervisor offers money; the awards can stack.

06

Plan proof of funds

Your study permit still needs proof of funds. A funding letter from the university can count toward it.
