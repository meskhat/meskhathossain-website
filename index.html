About me

I learned it the hard way, so you don't have to.

I came to Canada from Bangladesh as an international student. Finding work, finding a place to live, learning to cook, missing family, and still keeping up with classes. None of it was easy.

In 2021 I started The Human Changer to share what I was going through, honestly and in Bangla. Today it's a community of students and families who use my videos to plan one of the biggest decisions of their lives.

How can I go to Canada from Bangladesh as a student?

বাংলাদেশ থেকে স্টুডেন্ট হিসেবে কানাডায় কিভাবে যাব?

Choose a Designated Learning Institution (DLI), get your letter of acceptance, prepare your English test (usually IELTS) and proof of funds, then apply for a study permit through IRCC.

Watch: 3 things you must know to study in Canada →

How much money do I need to study in Canada?

কানাডায় পড়তে কত টাকা লাগে?

You need your first year's tuition plus proof that you can cover living costs. IRCC sets the minimum for living costs and updates it from time to time, so check their site for the current figure.

Watch: How much money you need to come to Canada →

Can I study in Canada with a low GPA?

কম GPA নিয়ে কি কানাডায় পড়া যায়?

Yes. Many Canadian colleges look at your overall profile, English score and study plan, not GPA alone.

Watch: Studying abroad with a low GPA →

Why do Canadian study permits get refused?

কানাডার ভিসা কেন বাতিল হয়?

Common reasons include weak or unclear financial documents, a study plan that doesn't fit your background, and doubts that you'll follow the permit's terms.

Watch: Why Canadian visas get refused →

How do I prepare for IELTS at home?

ঘরে বসে IELTS এর প্রস্তুতি কিভাবে নিব?

Practise all four skills every day with real test material, time yourself, and get feedback on your writing and speaking.

Watch: How to prepare for IELTS at home →

How do I get my first job in Canada?

কানাডায় প্রথম জব কিভাবে পাব?

Build a Canadian-style resume, apply widely for part-time and entry-level roles, network, and be ready to start with any job that gives you Canadian experience.

Watch: How to get your first job in Canada →

What is the PGWP?

PGWP কী?

The Post-Graduation Work Permit lets eligible graduates of Canadian institutions work in Canada after their studies. Eligibility rules change, so confirm them with IRCC.
