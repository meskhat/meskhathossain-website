Your questions, answered.

What Bangladeshi students ask me most, with the videos where I explain each one in Bangla.

Rules change often. Always confirm details on the official IRCC website.

How can I go to Canada from Bangladesh as a student?

বাংলাদেশ থেকে স্টুডেন্ট হিসেবে কানাডায় কিভাবে যাব?

Get a letter of acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution, get a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL) in most cases, prepare proof of identity and proof of funds, then apply for a study permit through IRCC.

Watch: 3 things you must know to study in Canada →

How much money do I need to study in Canada?

কানাডায় পড়তে কত টাকা লাগে?

For applications on or after September 1, 2026, a single applicant must show CAN$23,448 for a year of living costs (outside Quebec), plus tuition and travel.

Read: Proof of funds guide →

How many hours can I work as a student?

স্টুডেন্ট হিসেবে সপ্তাহে কত ঘণ্টা কাজ করা যায়?

Eligible students can work up to 24 hours a week off campus during classes, and unlimited hours during scheduled breaks.

Read: The 24-hour work rule →

Can I study in Canada with a low GPA?

কম GPA নিয়ে কি কানাডায় পড়া যায়?

Yes. Many Canadian colleges look at your overall profile, English score and study plan, not GPA alone.

Watch: Studying abroad with a low GPA →

Why do Canadian study permits get refused?

কানাডার ভিসা কেন বাতিল হয়?

Common reasons include weak or unclear financial documents, a study plan that doesn't fit your background, and doubts that you'll follow the permit's terms.
