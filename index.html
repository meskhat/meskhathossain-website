About

I came to Canada as an international student and learned everything the hard way. Now I share it, so the next student doesn't have to.

Being an international student is tough. You're looking for work, finding a place to live, learning to cook, missing family, and still trying to keep up with classes, all while competing with people who have far more experience.

In 2021 I started The Human Changer to share what I was going through, honestly and in Bangla. It has grown into a community of 32,000+ subscribers who use my videos to plan one of the biggest decisions of their lives.
