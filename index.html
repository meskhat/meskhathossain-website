About me

Hi, I'm Meskhat 👋

I came to Canada from Bangladesh as an international student, and quickly learned how tough it is. Finding a job, finding a place to live, learning to cook, missing family, and still keeping up with classes.

I started The Human Changer in 2021 to share what I was learning, honestly and in Bangla, so the next student has an easier start than I did.
