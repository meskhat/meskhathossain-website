My story

Learning it the hard way

Being an international student is tough. You're finding a job, finding a place to live, learning to cook, missing your family, and still trying to keep up with classes, often while competing with people who have far more experience.

I went through all of it. I studied at Centennial College in Toronto, worked my first jobs as a student, and later moved to Halifax, where I found work and received my post-graduation work permit in just 12 days.

Along the way I kept getting the same questions from students back home: How much money do I need? Which colleges are affordable? How do I get my first job? So I started answering them on YouTube.

"I help people in need, even if they can't do anything for me."
