Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

This website doesn't sell your data and doesn't run ads. I use Google Analytics to understand which guides and tools are helpful, and a Google Form to collect emails from people who ask for the free checklist.

Analytics and cookies

I use Google Analytics 4 to see things like how many people visit, which pages they read, which country they visit from, and which links they click. This helps me decide what guides to write next.

  • When you first visit, you can accept or decline analytics cookies.
  • If you decline (or haven't chosen yet), Google Analytics runs in a limited, cookieless mode and no analytics cookies are stored on your device.
  • Advertising features are turned off. Your data isn't used for ads by this site.
  • You can change your choice at any time: .

Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Email signups

If you request the free checklist, your first name and email address are stored in a Google Form that only I can access. I use them to send the checklist link and occasional emails about new guides. You can unsubscribe any time by replying "stop" or contacting me, and I'll delete your details.

Student tools

The budget calculator, checklist and timeline save your entries only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing you type into them is sent to me. The live exchange rate is loaded from ExchangeRate-API without sending any of your data.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Third-party content

Video thumbnails load from YouTube's image servers, fonts from Google Fonts, and the IRCC news list from the Government of Canada's news feed. Links to other websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

Questions

Email meskhathossain@gmail.com with any privacy question or to request access to or deletion of your information.
