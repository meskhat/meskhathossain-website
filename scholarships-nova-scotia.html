Bachelor's scholarships in Nova Scotia.

Every university below offers entrance scholarships that international students, including students from Bangladesh, can receive. Many are automatic: apply for admission early with good grades and you're considered.

8universitiesFeb 15earliest deadlineNov 2026last checked
1

Apply early. Scholarships usually need a complete admission application, transcripts included, by Feb 15 or Mar 1.

2

Grades decide. Most awards use your admission average. 80%+ opens doors, and 85%+ at StFX means a guaranteed $5,000 or more.

3

Fill in extra forms. Dalhousie and Saint Mary's have a separate entrance award application. Don't skip it.

From Bangladesh

Give yourself the best chance

01

Apply between Oct and Jan

Fall 2027 applications are already open. Applying by January leaves time to send transcripts before the Feb 15 or Mar 1 scholarship dates.

02

Send your grades early

Upload your SSC and HSC (or O and A Level) results, or your latest marks if HSC isn't finished. Each university converts them to its own average.

03

Look beyond the big name

Smaller universities like Acadia, StFX, CBU and MSVU give automatic awards that strong international students can receive.

04

Write the extra essays

The biggest awards (McKenna, Irving, Wright) ask about leadership and service. Start collecting examples from school, clubs and volunteering.

05

Check renewal rules

Awards paid "over 4 years" usually need a minimum GPA each year to keep. Read the renewal rules in your offer.

06

Budget for the gap

Even with a scholarship, you'll need proof of funds for your study permit. Use the budget calculator to plan the rest.
