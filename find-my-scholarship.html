Step by step

How to win a scholarship in Canada

What works for students from Bangladesh, at every level.

01

Apply early

Most scholarships need a complete admission application, transcripts included, by the deadline. Aim to apply by January.

02

Know what's automatic

Bachelor's entrance awards are often automatic. Graduate awards usually need a form, references or a supervisor's nomination.

03

Grades come first

Most awards rank by admission average or GPA. Send SSC/HSC or your full university transcript early so they can be converted.

04

Find a supervisor

For a research master's or PhD, a professor's support unlocks stipends and nominations. Use Find My Professor.

05

Write strong essays

Big awards ask about leadership, service and research. Collect examples from clubs, volunteering, jobs and projects now.

06

Read the renewal rules

Multi-year awards need a minimum GPA each year. Check the conditions in your offer before you accept.
