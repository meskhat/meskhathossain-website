PhD scholarships in Nova Scotia.

PhD students in Canada are usually funded. Your package combines a supervisor stipend, teaching work and scholarships. The awards below are the big ones international students can win in Nova Scotia.

$42Ktop yearly awardOct 16Dal round 1Nov 2026last checked

Dalhousie University

Halifax, Nova Scotia

Round 1: Oct 16, 2026

Dalhousie offers most of Nova Scotia's PhD programs and has large PhD scholarships open to international students. A second round opens Dec 1, 2026 and closes Jan 31, 2027. Apply on the Faculty of Graduate Studies scholarship platform.

Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Doctoral

Federal (any NS university), Nova Scotia

Agency: Oct 17

Canada's national PhD scholarship (it replaced the old CGS-D and Vanier). Up to 15% of awards go to international students, but you must already be registered in your PhD at a Canadian university by the deadline, so apply in your first or second year.

  • CGRS – Doctoral (NSERC / SSHRC / CIHR)Universities set earlier internal deadlines, e.g. SMU: Oct 19, 2026
    $40,000 a year, 3 yearsApply ↗

Not for students who haven't started their PhD yet. Plan for it in year one.

Saint Mary's University

Halifax, Nova Scotia

Apply by Jan 31, 2027

SMU has PhD programs in areas such as astronomy, applied science, business and international development. New PhD students can apply for the Durland Graduate Research Scholarship. The rest usually comes from your supervisor.

From Bangladesh

Give yourself the best chance

01

Find your supervisor

Shortlist professors with Find My Professor, read their recent papers, and email the ones whose work fits yours.

02

Ask about the package

Ask how much the total yearly funding is, for how many years, and whether it covers international tuition.

03

Apply to the big awards

Killam and DRES need strong grades, research experience and leadership. Your supervisor and department help you apply.

04

Publish if you can

Papers, conference posters and research jobs make scholarship applications much stronger.

05

Plan year-one CGRS

Once enrolled, apply for the $40,000 CGRS Doctoral through your university's internal deadline.

06

Keep proof of funds

A funding letter from the university helps with your study permit's proof of funds.
